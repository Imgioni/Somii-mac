# 002 by SPKR: cover image prompt (DALL·E)

## Main prompt

A square album-style cover for a synthesizer sound pack. The mood is minimal, premium and
editorial, like a high-end hardware synth product shot.

- **Subject:** an extreme close-up of a vintage-inspired analog synthesizer panel, photographed at
  a low 30-degree angle. Show:
  - a row of cream-coloured rotary knobs with fine engraved tick marks;
  - two tall slim faders with charcoal caps;
  - a single glowing orange indicator LED.
- **Materials:** a warm off-white panel (#E7E2DA) with a subtle matte powder-coat texture. The
  sub-panels are deep charcoal (#262627 to #424243). One accent colour only, a vivid orange
  (#F65A27), used on a thin printed section line and the LED glow.
- **Lighting:** soft top-left studio light, gentle falloff into shadow on the right, shallow depth
  of field with the front knob in crisp focus, and a faint warm bloom around the orange LED.
- **Composition:**
  - The panel fills the lower two-thirds.
  - Leave generous calm negative space in the upper-left third, in the cream panel tone, for a
    title.
  - Clean geometric balance, no clutter.
- **Style:** photorealistic product photography, Swiss / Bauhaus minimal, muted and sophisticated,
  high detail, 8k.
- **Avoid:** no text, no logos, no letters, no watermark, no people, no neon, no rainbow colours.

## Add the title yourself

Image generators misspell text, so put the title on afterwards in Photoshop, Canva or Figma, in
the empty upper-left area:

- **"002"**: large, bold condensed sans-serif (Bahnschrift Bold Condensed, or DIN Condensed /
  Barlow Condensed), charcoal #262627.
- **"BY S·P·K·R"**: small, wide letter-spacing, with a thin vertical rule between it and "002",
  the same as the plugin's panel mark.
- **Optional:** a small orange (#F65A27) line under the title, or a subtitle like
  "SOUND PACK VOL. 1" in small caps.

## Variations (swap into the main prompt)

- **Dark version:** "…panel in deep charcoal (#262627) with cream knobs and the same single orange
  accent; moody low-key lighting…"
- **Top-down flat lay:** "…shot straight from above, perfectly flat, knobs and faders arranged in a
  clean grid, like a technical diagram come to life…"
- **Abstract:** "…macro shot of a single cream knob and its engraved tick scale, the orange LED
  blurred into a soft glow in the background…"
